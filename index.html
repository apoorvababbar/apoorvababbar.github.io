Bio

Apoorva Babbar is a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Calgary, expected to graduate in 2027. She holds a Master's in Economics from the Delhi School of Economics and a Bachelor of Arts in Economics from Hansraj College, University of Delhi.

In Michaelmas 2025 she was a Visiting Researcher at the Saïd Business School, University of Oxford, hosted by Professor Renée Adams. Her work has been supported by a Mitacs Globalink Research Award and the Eyes High International Doctoral Scholarship, and she has presented at the Society of Labor Economists and Canadian Economic Association conferences.

Research Interests

Apoorva's research sits at the intersection of labor economics, the economics of innovation and gender. Her projects ask how financial markets react to news about artificial intelligence and whether the gender of a firm's CEO shapes that reaction, how public R&D spending during the Space Race affected the automation of manufacturing, and how gendered work norms and migration affect female labor force participation.

Teaching Interests

Apoorva has taught Principles of Macroeconomics as a sessional instructor and has been a teaching assistant for courses in microeconomics, macroeconomics, engineering economics, and managerial and decision economics. She received the Victor Yang Song Award for Teaching Excellence from the Department of Economics in 2024.
