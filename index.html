Bio

Apoorva Babbar is a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Calgary, expected to graduate in 2027. She holds a Master's in Economics from the Delhi School of Economics and a Bachelor's in Economics from the University of Delhi. She was a Visiting Researcher at Oxford's Saïd Business School in Fall 2025 and has presented her work at the Society of Labor Economists and Canadian Economic Association Conferences. She is on the 2026-27 job market.

Research Interests

Her research studies gender and inequality in labor markets, with a focus on innovation and artificial intelligence. Her job market paper examines if gender differences exist in AI leadership. She examines whether financial markets respond differently to AI news at male-led and female-led AI-exposed firms.

Teaching Interests

Apoorva has taught Principles of Macroeconomics as a sessional instructor and has been a teaching assistant for courses in microeconomics, macroeconomics, engineering economics, and managerial and decision economics. She received the Victor Yang Song Award for Teaching Excellence from the Department of Economics in 2024.
